# <Language name> (`<code>`)

Template for a new language layer. Copy to `<code>.md` (ISO 639-1, e.g. `pt.md`, `it.md`), write it in that language, and keep the same section order so the workflow finds each part. `es.md` and `en.md` are worked examples.

## Metadata

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Code | `<code>` |
| Variants | `<code>-<variant>` (default), ... |
| Speaking rate | ~N words per minute (range) |
| Voice files | `voice/samples/<code>-*` |

## Performance-mark labels

Bracket labels in this language for: beat intention (a verb), visual, on-screen text, pace, tone, sound, cut, fact to verify, anecdote to fill. The `/`, `//` and `**bold**` symbols are universal.

## Language-specific AI tells

What machine text sounds like in this language, beyond the universal catalog:

- AI / brochure vocabulary, with plain replacements.
- Essay connectors nobody says out loud.
- Calques from English (models often think in English and translate).
- Worn-out creator hooks and transitions.
- Register errors (e.g., a generic "dubbing" register that ignores the variant).
- What not to flag: openers, fillers and structures that generic humanizers mark as AI but are natural in this spoken language.

## Orality

Pronunciation traps, tongue twisters, how numbers, dates, symbols and acronyms are said, natural tenses and verb forms for speech, contractions or elisions, punctuation that helps intonation.

## Punctuation

Where commas and periods are required by this language's norm and where they're forbidden; question and exclamation marks (opening marks, if the language has them); quotation-mark style for quotes, voice changes and irony; which written-only signs to avoid in a script.

## Craft

How `../craft.md` applies with this language's resources: how to put the stress word at the end (word order or structure), the tense storytellers use for the key moment, how direct speech is introduced, the register of strong everyday verbs, idioms that can be twisted, and what this language's great speakers and storytellers do. Also list its stock images.

## Variants

One subsection per variant: address forms, vocabulary, fillers, and the caricature to avoid (overusing local slang is as fake as the neutral register).

## Before and after

Three levels of one short opening: AI, correct but flat, and eloquent, using this file's labels, with a line on why the third works.
